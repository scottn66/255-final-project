# 1.40 Risks and out of scope

**Layer:** Plan · **Up:** [1.00](1.00-plan.md) · **Prev:** [1.30](1.30-experiment.md) · **Across:** [2.00 Board](2.00-board.md) · **Spec:** PLAN.md A.5

## Three risks (plan these, do not “fix” with extra layers)

1. **Variable C.** `Conv2d(C, D, 1)` and `Linear(C·P², D)` break or silently reshape when C changes. Mitigation: tied ops + masked mean. Test: same module, C=32 then C=20.
2. **Spatial-split leakage.** 16×16 train patches overlapping a test block fake high OA. Mitigation: discard crossing patches. Never random-pixel split.
3. **PE scale on λ in nm.** Raw 400–2500 saturates sinusoids. Mitigation: divide by 1000 (microns). Test: 800 nm closer to 810 nm than to 2100 nm.
