## One sentence

A ~0.5M dual-path model that **degrades less than a vanilla ViT** when spectral bands are dropped or swapped.

## What this project is

**SSFT-Wave** reimplements a tiny Spectral–Spatial Fusion Transformer **from scratch**, replaces integer band-index embeddings with wavelength-aware PE, and tests robustness under band drop/swap.

| Paper | Use |
|-------|-----|
| [SSFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15828) §3 (Musiat, Ebert, Wasenmüller, Apr 2026) | **Implement this architecture.** No official code. **Not** SSFTT (TGRS 2022). |
| [LESSViT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18541) §2 (Si et al., May 2026) | **Steal two ideas only:** tied/channel-agnostic projection, wavelength PE. Do not reproduce LESS Attention, HyperMAE, or the 96-hour pretrain. |

Planning session 1 already wrote the full extract in PLAN.md. These `1.xx` notes are the group-facing map.
