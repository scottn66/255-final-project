## Split

Spatial **block** split (default 5×5 cells on Indian Pines). A 16×16 patch is train only if its **center** is in a train cell **and** its support does not hit val/test. No random-pixel API.

## Train once, test four ways

Train on **one frozen** in-distribution band subset (LESSViT-style split at **1000 nm**; Pro-VNIR mix). Save indices **and** nm.

| Test | What it means |
|------|----------------|
| ID | Same subset |
| Complementary / swapped | Leftover bands, similar C |
| Random ~50% drop | Subset of ID bands, 3 seeds |
| Optional full-C | All bands at test |

**Models:** vanilla ViT vs SSFT-index vs SSFT-Wave.

**Metrics:** OA, **AA**, **macro-F1**. Headline = relative drop `(ID − OOD) / ID` on AA and macro-F1. OA alone is a trap (Soybean-mintill 2455 vs Oats 20).

**Do not claim SOTA.**
